For health professionals

Sommelle is a free, open source Android app that supports adults with insomnia using the components of cognitive behavioral therapy for insomnia (CBT-I). It is designed as an educational and self-help tool, without claiming any therapeutic effect.

Download the printable sheet (PDF, in French) Read the scientific dossier

Status. Sommelle is a self-help and education tool, not a medical device, and claims no therapeutic effect. The protocol has not yet been evaluated in a large clinical study.

Program components

WeekComponent
0Daily sleep diary (Consensus Sleep Diary, Core version), sleep education
1Sleep restriction (time in bed), stimulus control
2 to 5Cognitive exercises: beliefs about sleep, constructive worry (putting the day to rest), paradoxical intention, decatastrophizing, progressive muscle relaxation
6Before/after review: observation week compared with the last 7 nights

Sleep restriction

  • 7-day baseline; at least 5 of 7 nights logged to calculate or adjust a window.
  • Initial window: mean total sleep time at baseline, rounded up to the next quarter hour.
  • Floor of 5 h 30 min (6 h for activities at risk from drowsiness), never below 5 h; ceiling of 9 h.
  • Fixed rise time chosen by the patient, 7 days a week; the bedtime follows from it.
  • Weekly adjustment: sleep efficiency of at least 85%: +15 min; below 80%: −15 min; in between: unchanged. Thresholds lowered by 5 points from age 65.
  • Weekly efficiency calculated on totals (sum of time asleep / sum of time in bed).

Safety and referral

An entry questionnaire leads to one of three pathways: full program, program without restriction, or referral to a doctor.

Reported situationAction
Snoring with breathing pauses, marked daytime sleepinessNo restriction, medical referral
Bipolar disorder, epilepsyNo restriction, referral to the treating doctor
PregnancyNo restriction
Driving for work, machinery, safety-critical jobFloor raised to 6 h and stronger warning, or no restriction, as the patient chooses
Very late schedule, normal sleep once asleepNo restriction, referral (possible delayed sleep phase)
Shift or night work, nights interrupted by someone else, under 18Out of scope, referral
Distress expressedCrisis line highlighted (3114 in France)

A warning about drowsy driving is shown at the start of the program. The details of each rule, with a worked example, are in the scientific dossier.

Design

  • Diary entries by estimate, without timing, so as not to encourage clock watching.
  • No daily score or streak to maintain, to limit the risk of orthosomnia.
  • Interface designed for older people and those less comfortable with technology: large text, big buttons, plain language.
  • No data collected: everything stays on the patient's phone.
  • The patient can show you a summary on screen, or share it as a PDF (program, pathway, weekly table, before/after review, last 28 nights) and as a spreadsheet (full diary).
