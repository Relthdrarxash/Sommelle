Sommelle is an application dedicated to treating chronic insomnia through first-line Cognitive Behavioral Therapy for Insomnia (CBT-I), recommended by the European Sleep Research Society (ESRS 2023) and the American Academy of Sleep Medicine (AASM 2021).

Fully open-source (MPL-2.0), free, ad-free, without subscriptions, accounts, or telemetry, and 100% offline: it does not even request the Android INTERNET permission. Your private sleep data never leaves your device.

A STRUCTURED 6-WEEK PROTOCOL
• Clinical intake screening: Evaluates contraindications for sleep restriction (sleep apnea, bipolar disorder, epilepsy, pregnancy, safety-critical occupation) and assigns an appropriate pathway.
• Week 1 observation: Quick daily sleep diary completed in twenty seconds without typing, calculating your baseline metrics (mean time in bed, total sleep time, initial sleep efficiency).
• Educational transition buffer: Clear review of your baseline metrics and protocol rationale before adjusting time in bed.
• Weeks 2 to 5 consolidation: Calibrated time-in-bed restriction with a regular wake-up anchor, stimulus control, and progressive cognitive modules (addressing sleep anxiety, sleep hygiene, defusing racing thoughts).
• Week 6 outcome review: Objective before/after comparison of your sleep efficiency and daytime functioning.

CLINICAL TOOLS INCLUDED
• Standardized sleep diary inspired by the Consensus Sleep Diary (CSD).
• Ultra-low brightness "Can't sleep" night mode to guide stimulus control without circadian disruption.
• Local PDF and CSV clinical export to share with your physician or sleep specialist.
• 100% offline, privacy-first, zero tracking.
