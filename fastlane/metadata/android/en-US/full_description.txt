Do you struggle to fall asleep, wake up during the night or too early, and has it been going on for a while? Sommelle guides you for six to eight weeks with cognitive behavioral therapy for insomnia (CBT-I), the first-line method recommended by European and American insomnia guidelines, before sleeping pills.

HOW IT WORKS
• A few questions at the start, to check that the program is right for you and point you to a doctor if needed.
• An observation week: each morning, you log your night in about twenty seconds. An estimate is enough.
• Your sleep window: a bedtime and a rise time, adjusted every week based on your nights.
• Support at night: if you can't sleep, a button reminds you what to do, on a dark screen, with no numbers or clock.
• A review every week, explained in one sentence, with a simple chart.

DESIGNED FOR EVERYONE
• Large text, big buttons, plain language.
• No clock dial: big buttons to move forward or back by 15 minutes.
• Built for TalkBack and large text sizes.
• A warm, dark night theme, automatic in the evening.

YOUR DATA STAYS WITH YOU
• No account, no ads, no trackers.
• The app has no Internet access: nothing is sent.
• Free and open source: the code is public and anyone can check it.

GOOD TO KNOW
Sommelle is a self-help and education tool, not a medical device. It does not replace medical advice. It is designed for adults; it is not suited to night work or to nights interrupted by someone else (a baby, a relative who needs help). If you snore with pauses in breathing or fall asleep during the day, talk to your doctor.

If you are going through a hard time, call your local emergency number or a crisis line in your country. In France, 3114 is free, day and night.
